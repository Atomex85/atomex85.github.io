Who is Hazefall

Tomi

// Finland

Hazefall is a Finnish electronic music artist crafting immersive house and dance tracks that blur the line between atmosphere and dancefloor energy.

Drawing inspiration from the Nordic landscape and the raw pulse of underground club culture, Hazefall builds soundscapes that feel both vast and intimate.

Based in Finland, released through DistroKid — available on all major platforms.
